About Cheri

A trusted guide to South Florida's finest addresses.

Cheri knows South Florida is one of the most amazing places in the world to live or vacation. Whether it's a condo on the beach, a single family residence in Boca Raton, a winter getaway in Palm Beach, or a country club estate in a golfing or yachting community, she tunes in to each of her clients' dreams while preserving an absolute commitment to meeting their individual needs.

A native of the Northeast, Cheri has maintained residences in New Jersey, Upstate New York, Maryland, and New York City — which means she's familiar with the transition from city or suburb to oceanfront buying, whether for a vacation or primary residence. That knowledge has greatly enhanced her ability to match clients with their dreams.

For sellers, Cheri takes full advantage of Lang Realty's marketing tools to ensure properties are broadly promoted on every major channel. For buyers, she analyzes the local market to find the perfect home at the right price.
